Installation Guide

Follow these simple steps to install Julex manually in your browser.

1

Download & Extract

Download the latest .ZIP package from our download page. Once downloaded, extract the contents of the ZIP file to a folder on your computer.

[ ZIP Extraction Preview ]
2

Open Extensions Page

Open your browser and navigate to chrome://extensions (or the equivalent for your browser). You can also find this in the menu under More Tools > Extensions.

[ Extensions Page Preview ]
3

Enable Developer Mode

In the top right corner of the Extensions page, toggle the "Developer mode" switch to the ON position. This will reveal new options.

[ Developer Mode Toggle ]
4

Load Unpacked

Click the "Load unpacked" button and select the folder where you extracted the Julex files in Step 1. The extension will be installed immediately!

[ Load Unpacked Button ]

💡 Pro Tip

Pin Julex to your toolbar for quick access to all its features. Click the puzzle icon in your browser toolbar and click the pin icon next to Julex.
