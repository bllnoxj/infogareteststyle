Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Commitment to Privacy

At Julex, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Our extension is built with a "Privacy by Design" approach. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, browsing history, or information entered into the extension.

Data Collection

Julex does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not track your browsing history.
  • We do not collect your IP address.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking pixels.
  • We do not have any external analytics or telemetry scripts.

Permissions Usage

To provide its features, Julex requires certain browser permissions. Here is how they are used:

  • Storage: Used solely to save your local settings and preferences within your browser's local storage.
  • Tabs: Used to provide tab management features. This information never leaves your browser.
  • ActiveTab: Allows the extension to interact with the current page only when you explicitly click on a tool.

Third-Party Services

Julex does not communicate with any third-party services or servers. All operations are performed locally on your computer. If you choose to use an optional feature that requires an external API, you will be explicitly asked for consent and your API keys will be stored locally.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository.
